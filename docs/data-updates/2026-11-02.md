- `Claw X-MOD2` explains how to climb to the top of the pyramid, identifies the weapon as an axe, and includes one upstream image.
- `Knight of Pentacles` is documented as Minor Arcana on a rock wall face.
- `Page of Cups` is placed on the north-facing wall of the Chubby Buffalo restaurant.
- `John Wick reference` describes the crate beside the shop owner and includes one upstream image.
- The four new Dogtown airdrops include tag `119`; two have short placement descriptions and two have no description.
- `Blade Runner reference` currently has no detail record upstream.

## Main-job navigation changes

The new records extend the Phantom Liberty job navigation in two branches:

- `Run This Town` → `The Killing Moon` → `Unfinished Sympathy` → `From Her to Eternity`
- `Firestarter` → `Black Steel in the Hour of Chaos` → `Somewhat Damaged` → `Leave in Silence`

`Firestarter` now links forward to both `Run This Town` and `Black Steel in the Hour of Chaos`. Upstream currently leaves the `Next` field empty on `Black Steel in the Hour of Chaos`, even though `Somewhat Damaged` links back to it; this asymmetry is preserved.
